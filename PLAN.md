# Six-day plan

Exam: **15 Nov 2026**. Start: **9 Nov**. Budget: about 2 hours a day.

## Daily loop

| Block | Time | What |
|---|---:|---|
| New cards | 60 min | One domain. Answer, read the trap, move. |
| Cheatsheet | 20 min | The matching section only. Cover the right column, recall, check. |
| Missed drill | 30 min | The drill queue from the session summary. |
| Recall | 10 min | Say the three hardest formulas out loud, no notes. |

Rules:

- One pass through a new deck per day. Do not reread.
- A card you miss comes back in the same session. Do not stop to study it.
- A deck is done when the drill queue is empty, not when the cards run out.

## Exam day

- Read the cover page for the scoring rule first.
- Two passes. Pass 1 takes the items you can finish in 30 seconds.
- Mark every item if wrong answers are not penalized.
- Last 5 minutes: transfer answers and check numbering. Start no new solution.

## What this plan does not cover

- Essay or interview stages. This reviewer is for the written examination only.
- Memorizing past items. DOST-SEI does not release them. The cards teach the domain, not a leaked paper.
